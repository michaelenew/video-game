# Shifter skills

## Rework

*Aesthetic idea — the shifter's weapon appears as elaborate metal gloves that cover their forearms until used.*

- **Rush** — passive that allows the shifter to charge up a dash over short period, only 1 charge maximum at a time but charging fully allows the dash to be performed at any time; shift click to charge
- **Sword** — auto becomes a medium range melee strike; all abilities castable while rushing, but charge does no damage itself
  - **Uppercut** — damaging attack that throws the sword into the air, sword reaches its apex quickly and spins in place for a bit before falling
- **Hammer** — auto becomes a short melee blow; auto while rushing to stagger
  - **Hammerfall** — slams hammer into ground, staggering and damaging nearby foes; damage and stagger increase with the downward velocity of the shifter while casting
  - Parrying gives a stacking increased resistance buff
- **Spear** — auto becomes a long melee thrust; auto the ground while moving or rushing to vault
  - **Spear toss** — throws the spear at high speed

## Skills

- **Bull rush** — charges forward, pushing all enemies hit with him and stunning them briefly upon the end of the skill
- **Determination** — increases defensive stats based on number of enemies
- **Desperate strength** — increases offensive stats based on missing health
- **Safeguard** — shield; weapon returns to the shifter, deal damage to anything along the way
- **Rending uppercut** — axe; slashes violently upward, dealing damage to enemies in front, throws axe upward at the end of swing
- **Whirlwind slash** — greatsword; next three autos within a few seconds gain increased damage and slash in a diagonally downward cut while moving forward, leaping into the air and dealing even more damage
- **Raise shield** — shield; instantly raises shield, scroll back to brace and block more damage
- **Downward blow** — shield → unarmed; hits the ground, causing a shockwave that knocks enemies back
