# Monster design -- the standard every creature is built to

What a creature has to be before it stands in a place anybody can load.
The contract every fight is held to -- fair, telegraphed, a loss the
player's -- is the [bestiary](bestiary.md)'s and is not repeated here; this
is what a new creature has to have and how it is checked. The process is
[delivery.md](delivery.md); places are [level-design.md](level-design.md).

**Every rule here holds unless otherwise instructed.** An instruction for a
particular piece of work wins over any of them; where one is set aside, the
report says which and why ([delivery.md](delivery.md), D6).

## The rules

**M1 · A creature is whole before it is shown in play.** Unless otherwise
instructed, a creature stands in a loadable place only once it has, at the
least: a body that collides; parts that can be hit, with the soft places
and the armoured ones it is designed with; one move or more with a
telegraph; a way of noticing a fighter and turning to them; and a fight the
scripted hunter can play (`cargo run -p hunt --bin fight`). Less than that
is a picture, and pictures stay in screenshot sets
([level-design.md](level-design.md), L9).

**M2 · The bestiary's contract.** Unless otherwise instructed, every
threat is announced before it lands, every opening it gives is real, and
the hunt report's *unanswerable* count is zero ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md)
§1).

**M3 · One line for the fight, and a number for it.** Unless otherwise
instructed, a creature's document says in one line what the fight is and
the approach it teaches (as the bestiary's cast table does), and names what
the harness should show if that line is true -- the share of hits landed
from above, say, for a fight that is meant to be won off the ground.

**M4 · Every class has a way to do real damage.** Unless otherwise
instructed, a fight is winnable by each class on offer with its whole kit,
melee and ranged alike, even if it is easier for some. Where the damage is
only open from somewhere (on its back, from above), each class has a way
to get there or to reach it from where it can be.

**M5 · Size against the whole kit.** Unless otherwise instructed, a
creature's height and the height of its soft places are set against what
the five movers reach with everything they have
([level-design.md](level-design.md), L7), and the numbers are written down.

**M6 · Tested before it is tuned by hand.** Unless otherwise instructed,
the hunter's report (wins, fight length, what landed, the unanswerable
count) is in the creature's document before the owner is asked to play it,
and the owner's time goes on feel, not on finding that a move never lands.
