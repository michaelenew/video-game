# 0039 · The Bulwark: immovable, unstoppable

## The brief (the owner, 2026-11-11)

> Time to bring the bulwark back. Go figure out its design, theme, concept, etc

He was set aside on 2026-10-21 as "too far behind the other five, with no clear way yet to
make it fit", a day after he stopped being counted as a mover. This note looks for the way.
It is a proposal with one recommendation and two runners-up, written to be argued with.

## 1 · Why he fell behind

Three things, in the order they bit.

1. **His verb is waiting.** A block produces not-being-hit. v2 made it produce *weight*, which
   Slam, the throw and (0012) his two height moves spend. That fixed "why block", and moved the
   problem: everything good he has is behind having been hit first. Unload with an empty shield
   is "a stomp and barely a hop". The Blood mage had the same fault and 0038 named it: movement
   gated behind hurting somebody. His is gated behind being hurt.
2. **The game went up and he did not.** His full hop is 2.7 m, the lowest; the rest reach 4 to
   6 m and cross 50 m gaps with their kits. The owner's words in 0038: "the game has drifted
   more towards hypermobility as a necessary component". A wall wins by making people come to
   it, and nobody in this game has to.
3. **His other ground was taken while he sat out.** The wall is the Elementalist's stones. The
   grab is the Blood mage's Seize (2026-11-04), and so is the chain, which was his written
   fallback ("a grappler on a chain or a hook", bulwark-v2.md). The braced charge is the
   Champion's Rush. What is still nobody's: the shield itself, and being heavy.

One thing has changed in his favour. When he was designed there was nowhere worth standing.
Since 2026-10-21 there are hills to hold, five points to push, and floating stages where
knocking somebody off the edge wins. A class about holding ground and shoving has jobs now
that did not exist the day he was shelved.

## 2 · The profile he is held to

From 0037 and 0038, what the Reaver, the Elementalist and the Champion share, and the Dual
mage and the Blood mage were rebuilt to:

| | The test | The Bulwark today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Up, along, down and a hang, one owner each | Down (the Slam) and a hang (the Sail) are real. Up needs weight. **Along does not exist** |
| 2 | Separate budgets, so the order is free | One budget, the weight, and it is filled by the other player |
| 3 | A thing that comes out of the class too strong to stop, and it moves them | Nothing |
| 4 | Damage and movement in one act | The Battering ram, three metres of it |
| 5 | Momentum kept | None: every move ends standing |
| 6 | One simple thing, and the intent is how you use it | The shield could be this and is not: seven moves, seven rules |
| 7 | A use outside a fight | A wall to hide behind |

And 0038's two questions: **what is the one fact every move follows from**, and **why a
shield and not paint** -- what does a shield do that nothing else in the game does?

## 3 · The answer proposed: the shield does not stop a force, it turns it

A weapon is defined by what it does. A shield is the one tool defined by what *arrives* at
it. And it does not make what arrives disappear: the force goes somewhere. Into the ground,
back where it came from, or into the man behind it.

**So the law: nothing that reaches the shield is lost. He chooses where it goes.**

The old riddle is what happens when an unstoppable force meets an immovable object. He is
both, never anything between, and the shield is where one turns into the other:

- **Immovable.** Feet set, shield up. What hits him goes into the ground. Nothing shifts him,
  and nothing gets past: a dash that meets him stops. In a game where everybody else is
  motion, he is the one place motion ends.
- **Unstoppable.** The same force taken as speed instead. He goes down on the shield and
  rides it: a sled with two hundred pounds on it. He is slow to start and does not stop.
  What he meets takes his speed as the blow and goes with him.

Alone on a hillside there is still force to turn, which is what frees his movement: **gravity,
and his own weight.** A fall turned at the floor is speed along it. Speed turned at the
shield's edge, dug in, is height: a pole vault. Height is a harder Slam. One quantity -- how
fast he is going -- changing direction every time it meets the shield.

It answers "why a shield alone" without a meter: the shield is his vehicle, his springboard
and his weapon, and a blow he blocks is one more push.

### What follows from it

- **The battery becomes his speed.** A Bulwark moving fast *is* loaded, and it reads from
  across the arena better than a shield going dark. (Whether the stored weight survives beside
  it as power only is open, §7.)
- **Blocking is a launch.** [defense.md](../defense.md) already says a block costs space:
  knockback, and a moment stuck. For him alone the knockback is the payment. Braced, he does
  not move and the blow shakes the ground round him. Holding a direction, he rides the blow
  away as a slide, turns, and comes back as the ram.
- **He is the thing too strong to stop** (test 3). The Reaver sends a shadow, the
  Elementalist a stone. He sends himself.
- **Stopping is his biggest attack.** Digging the shield in at full speed puts all of it into
  the ground in front of him, and whoever he was carrying keeps going. At the lip of a
  floating stage that is the round.
- **He never floats.** He goes up by turning speed, and comes down like something dropped.
  The contrast with the Dual mage, who dances in the air, is the point of having both.

### The three places the shield can be

One object, and where he puts it is the intent (test 6):

| The shield is | He is | It turns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In front** (the face) | Guarding, braced or giving | A blow into the ground, or into speed |
| **Under him** (the sled) | Sliding | A fall or a slope into speed along the floor |
| **In the ground** (the edge) | Stopping or vaulting | Speed into a shake ahead, or into height |
| **Away** (thrown, planted) | On foot, lighter, exposed | Other people's motion: a wall that ends a dash, and a ramp for his own |

## 4 · A kit sketch

A shape to argue with, not a promise card. Numbers are from today's tuning where given.

| Input | On foot | On the sled | In the air |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shift` (the dodge) | **The kick-off**: his dodge throws him down onto the shield, and **holding it keeps him there**. Let go and he stands up | -- | The airdodge, as everyone's; held as he lands, he lands sliding and **keeps the fall as speed** |
| `L` -- the face | Bash | **The ram**: the blow is his speed, and a body it meets is carried on the face until he stops, turns hard or hits a wall | Rebound, as built |
| `M` -- the edge | Slam | Tapped, **the Stop**: every metre a second goes into a shake thrown ahead of him, and what he carried flies on. With `space`, **the Vault**: speed along becomes height | Slam, as built: lands with the feet, paid for the fall |
| `R` held -- the guard | **Braced**: immovable, parry in the opening frames. With a direction held when the blow lands, **he gives** and rides it | The shield comes up in front: he slides guarded, slower | The Sail, as built; a blow blocked up there throws him |
| `E` | Throw / recall / leap, as built. Planted, it is a wall, **a stop for anybody's dash, and a ramp for his slide** | | |
| `Q` | The Grapple, as built -- or folded into the carry (§7) | | |

**Up, along, down, hang:** the Vault, the slide, the Slam, the Sail. One owner each.

**What the speed is worth, roughly.** A vault that gave back all of his speed as height
would reach v²/2g. With his gravity (about 50 m/s²): at the dodge's 19 m/s, 3.6 m, a little
over his jump; at 25 m/s, 6.3 m, the Dual mage's hop; at the Champion's Rush speed of 32 m/s,
10 m. So the vault from a standing kick-off is ordinary and the vault after a hill or a drop
is the best single lift on the roster, and the difference is earned by the line he took.

**The loops.**

- *Alone, moving:* kick off → carve down the slope → off the crest → land on the sled and
  keep it → vault the gap → Slam on the far side, or land sliding.
- *Versus, attacking:* kick off → ram, and carry them → the Stop at the edge, or into the
  wall, or into his own planted shield.
- *Versus, holding a hill:* braced on the point. A dash at him ends on the shield. A blow he
  gives to carries him off the point at speed; he carves round and comes back through it.
- *A creature:* its blows are the biggest forces in the game. Braced, a stomp shakes the
  ground under its own feet. Given to, a stomp is the fastest he will ever go -- into a vault
  that puts him on its back, which is where [opening.md](../opening.md) says a big one is
  brought down from.

## 5 · Held against the profile

| | Test | Under the proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Four directions | Vault / slide / Slam / Sail |
| 2 | Separate budgets | **It breaks this one on purpose**, see below |
| 3 | Too strong to stop, and moves him | Himself, on the shield |
| 4 | Damage and movement in one act | The ram's blow is his speed |
| 5 | Momentum kept | It is the whole class |
| 6 | One thing, the intent in the use | The shield: in front, under, in the ground, away |
| 7 | Outside a fight | Sledding the valley; a ramp; a wall; later, a friend riding the shield |

**Test 2 is where this departs from the finished classes, and it is the first thing to
doubt.** Theirs draw on separate pools so any order works. His moves all draw on one, his
speed, and the order is forced: along before up. The case for it: the freedom moves from the
spending to the *sources*, which are separate -- the kick-off (the dodge's own budget), a
drop, a slope, a wall to rebound from, a blow given to -- and reading the ground for the next
one is his skill, where reading the air is the Dual mage's. The case against: on a flat
floor with nobody swinging at him he has one source, and that may read as one move. If so,
the vault wants a small budget of its own.

## 6 · Theme, and what he looks like

**Who he is.** The lore already written holds up: the new cities were villages that banded
together with no aristocracy and no duelling tradition. The Champion is a hired duellist.
The Bulwark is whoever in the village could carry the gate. Not a knight: a mason, a miller,
a dock hand. The heaviest frame on the roster, in working clothes with plate where the work
needs it, plausible in the way [cast-look.md](../cast-look.md) asks.

**The shield is a piece of the town.** A gate leaf: timber as tall as he is, banded in iron,
too big to be a soldier's. Big enough to stand behind, to stand *on*, and to ride. It could
carry a mark for each village that gave a plank to it.

**Where the pictures come from.**

- *The name.* Homer's word for Ajax, who carried a shield "like a tower", is the bulwark of
  the Achaeans. A bulwark is also a breakwater and the side of a ship: the thing a wave
  breaks on and is turned by.
- *The shield belongs to the line.* The saying Plutarch gives the Spartans: a man wears a
  helmet for his own sake and carries a shield for the whole line's. That is the communal
  defender in one sentence.
- *The sled is history.* Plutarch has the Cimbri crossing the Alps and sliding down the snow
  slopes on their shields. Breath of the Wild's shield surfing is the same joke, and
  everybody who sees it understands it at once.
- *The planted shield is history too*: the pavise, a tall shield stood on the ground as a
  wall.
- *Immovable, then unstoppable* is sumo: the set, the charge, and a win by putting the other
  man out of the ring.
- *From games.* Team Fortress 2's shield charge, where players learned to turn a charge up a
  ramp into flight; Tribes' skiing, speed kept by reading slopes; Overwatch's Wrecking Ball
  and Reinhardt, the tank who is the fastest thing on the map and the charge that pins;
  League's Poppy, whose whole job is stopping dashes; Monster Hunter's lance and gunlance,
  the guard that advances and the blast that carries. v2's battery was Monster Hunter's
  Charge Blade, which is worth knowing because it is a famously *complicated* weapon.

**The animation brief.** Low and wide. Every start shows the effort and every stop throws
earth. On the sled he is on one knee, or standing sideways on it at speed. He never hangs in
the air except behind the shield. Sound follows for free: [sound.md](../sound.md) reads a
blow's weight off its numbers, and his are the heaviest.

## 7 · Two other answers, and why they came second

**The fixed point.** The shield, once set, cannot be moved by anything, him included, and it
can be set in mid-air. Recall does not bring it to him; it brings him to it. He swings round
it, kicks off it, stands on it. *For:* the object exists in the air, and it is the cleanest
"immovable". *Against:* a solid you place is the Elementalist's stone, and flying to a thing
you placed is the Reaver's dash and the Blood mage's zip. It would be the third of each.

**Keep the battery, and let his own falls fill it.** The smallest change: v2 as built, with
landings and collisions storing weight so the height moves work alone. *For:* almost all of
it exists. *Against:* it is still a meter, still seven moves with seven rules, and he still
has nothing that goes along. It makes him adequate, which is what he was.

## 8 · Open, and what would change the answer

1. **Is this the class?** The one question that matters. If "immovable, unstoppable" is not
   it, §7's fixed point is the next to try.
2. **He becomes a mover.** The proposal reverses the 2026-10-20 rule. He would go back into
   `MOVERS` only after the movement search has measured what he reaches, and sections would
   still not be re-sized for him: he would cross them his own way or not at all.
3. **The stored weight.** Three choices: gone, his speed is the battery; kept as power only,
   the Dual mage's rule ("movement is the mage herself; power is behind the bars"), sizing
   the Slam's shake and the planted wall; or kept as now. Recommended: gone, then see what is
   missed.
4. **Moving the enemy.** The carry and the Stop push people about, and 0037 records that
   "push and pull on the enemy has been tried and does not work" for the Dual mage. If that
   was about her and not about shoving in general, this stands. If it was general, the ram
   hits and passes through instead of carrying, and he loses the ring-out job.
5. **The grab.** The Blood mage has Seize now. His Grapple could stay as the turtle's answer,
   or the carry could be his grab: a ram that takes a guarding body with it.
6. **Flat floors.** Most duel stages are boxes. Drops between levels and the kick-off have to
   be enough, or he is only himself in the valley.
7. **What it asks of the engine.** A slide over the land's seams (about 2,400 of them, steps
   or ledges since 2026-10-13) must hop them, not stop dead. A speed ceiling, so the reach is
   paid in execution and not free. The camera at 30 m/s. The carry, in a rollback snapshot.
   None looks impossible; none is costed.
8. **Throw and recall.** Kept as built here because they work. Whether a man whose shield is
   his vehicle ever wants to throw it away is a fair question once the rest is played.

If the direction is agreed, the next step is the promise card ([delivery.md](../delivery.md)
D7) and a first slice: the kick-off, the slide, the ram, the Stop and the Vault on the
shield he already has, measured by the movement search before anything else is touched.
