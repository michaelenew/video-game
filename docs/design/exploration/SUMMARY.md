| [0005](0005_body_plans.md) | **Can a body plan be bred?** The world thread wants creatures bred into lines that cross. This is the feasibility study for the body itself: six layers (knobs, proportions, parts, traits, moves, topology), what each is in code today, and what a gene at each would cost against the budgets. Finds that poses are angles so proportions need no re-bake, that three species share one skeleton so moves transplant within the family for free, that recipes are written against role traits so one shared trait would open the body across families, and that the `f32` solver means a bred clip is shipped as a table rather than baked on two machines. Proposes the body-parent rule for hybrids and the genome's body section; five experiments (B0 build vector, B1 part mask, B2 a cousin's move, B3 the role trait, B4 a hybrid in the harness), each with a measure and a stop | **Run 2026-10-03**, §7 and [0005_results.md](0005_results.md): every layer can change and nothing crashes when it does, which is the problem -- proportions lose their footing, route and aim; moves cross a skeleton family and lose what lived in the donor's hooks; across families copied angles keep the bones and lose the move; declared senses do nothing without their row; the bake agrees with itself and not between machines. Instrument: `crates/lab` |
| [0006](0006_differences_in_kind.md) | **Numbers as differences in kind.** Pre-registered: does moving a creature's knobs inside the Oven's ranges make a different fight, or only a harder one? Defines difficulty, a fight signature from the report, a seed-noise floor and a distance in noise units; four measures (the contract rate, separability at matched difficulty, identifiability from one run, a per-knob kind/degree/dead ranking with cliffs); three sampling regimes on the Ridgeback; thresholds and predictions written before the run; a `spread` tool beside `fight` and a standard-library analysis script. One hunt is about 80 ms in release, so the whole run is under two hours on four cores | **Running**: the tool, the baseline, the temper control and R1 and R2 are done; R3 (one knob at a time) is running |
| [0007](0007_the_gate.md) | **The gate.** What a generated creature must pass before it is born, written from what broke in 0005 and 0006: check function, not form. Six jobs a part can do (support, striker, surface, weak point, breakable, sensor) and seven layers of check, cheapest first -- the table is well formed, it stands, its blows come from its body, the route exists, what it declares it has, the fight holds, two machines agree -- each with the experiment that failed it. Ends with the four hard problems against the gate and what to build, in order | Open. Next: the re-bake test, derived per-limb tables, the trait declaration check |
