# 0008 — The Elementalist on three clicks

## The decision this note works from

From the person, on 0007:

- **The core of the class goes on the three clicks: her stones, her fire and her wind.**
  Left click raises stones, middle click is the Fire pillar, and right click is a new
  grounded wind move.
- **The Bolt and Cataclysm move off the clicks, onto `Q` and `E`.** The Bolt becomes the
  weak push and Cataclysm the strong push. The two keys become a matched pair: they push
  what she has built, gently or hard.
- **The new grounded wind move is an air ball**, like the air scooter in *Avatar: The Last
  Airbender*. Holding the button makes it bigger, faster and longer lasting. It is aimed with
  two points, a starting place and a direction, the way Fissure is. It is for moving things,
  herself or others, so it goes much further than her other moves and carries things along
  with it. It grows visibly while she charges it, then shrinks visibly at a steady rate as it
  travels, until it peters out.
- **The wind takeoff is the Updraft**, and it has to keep her sideways speed, so it
  stretches a jump rather than stopping it.
- **The Thermal stays.** That means the fire aerial has to be one she can lay out and then
  Updraft into, for a big boost.
- **The Gale stays as the wind aerial.** The fire aerial has to stay around long enough
  that a Gale fired straight after it goes through it and comes out lit, the stronger fire
  disc, in one quick combo.
- **The pillar (and the tornado Cataclysm makes of it) is still the grounded fire move.**
  The Flare dash is out. The fire aerial hangs in front of her as a red carpet or a wall:
  laid out first, then shot through or Updrafted into, for a boost forwards and upwards.
- **The earth takeoff and the earth aerial are the person's to design.** This note leaves
  them open and only says where they plug in.

**The symmetry.** Every click is now a tap and a hold. The tap is the move straight away,
and the hold is the same move made bigger while she crawls. The two keys are the two
strengths of push. The clicks are what she makes, and the keys are what she does to what
she made.

**What the playtest bar gets.** With only the three clicks she now has a stone that
erupts under somebody, a 175-damage pillar, and a ball that carries people where she sends
them. On foot she is no longer the one class that cannot fight with its clicks, which
answers 0007's CORE-4 better than moving her game into the air did.

## 2 · The Air ball: right click on foot

### What it does

1. **Press:** a ball of spinning air forms on the floor where the crosshair meets the
   ground. That is the starting place.
2. **Hold:** it grows, visibly, up to a cap. She crawls while she holds, as she does for
   the Strike and Fissure.
3. **Release:** it sets off, flat along the ground, towards where the crosshair meets the
   ground now. That is the direction. A tap is a small, quick ball. A full hold is a big
   one.
4. **Travelling:** it shrinks at a steady rate until it is gone.
5. **Anything inside it goes with it:** her, another fighter, a critter. It brings things
   along, which is the whole point of it.

### Why a steady shrink is the right rule

Because it shrinks at a fixed rate, **how long it lasts depends only on how big it is when
it sets off.** A ball twice the size lasts twice as long. If its speed also grows with its
size, then the distance it covers (speed × time) grows with the *square* of its size. So
twice the charge goes about four times as far. That is the "much further" the move asked
for, and it comes out of one rule rather than three separate knobs.

It also makes the ball **readable**. Anybody looking at it can tell how far it will go from
how big it is, and they can watch it running out.

### Carrying

- **A body is held at the ball's centre and moves at its speed.** When the ball has shrunk
  below the size of a body, it can no longer hold one: the body drops out with the ball's
  speed and the ball goes on, empty, until it is gone. So the ride ends a little before the
  ball vanishes. The player watches it peter out and gets put down, not dropped.
- **Riding it:** she makes the ball at her own feet and stands in it while she charges it.
  When she releases, she goes.
- **Jumping off keeps the ball's speed.** That is what stretches the ride into the air, and
  it is where the Updraft (§3) comes in.
- **Carrying somebody else:** they are carried and can jump out at any time, which is
  their way out. She decides where they are taken: into her pillar, against a stone, off a
  ledge. A creature too big to fit in the ball is not carried; it is shoved, the way the
  Gale shoves.
- **It follows the ground.** Off a ledge it falls like anything else, so it does not
  bridge gaps on the jump courses. That keeps it from making the courses trivial.
- **A wall or a stone stops it.** It bursts against them, and what it carried is let go.

### How it fits her algebra

The kit's rule from 0002 is that a verb does something different depending on what it
meets. The Air ball is a new thing on the field, so the two push keys need an answer for
it:

- **`Q`, the weak push, kicks it.** The Bolt hits the ball and redirects it towards
  wherever the Bolt was aimed, a little faster. It is how she steers a ride she has
  already sent.
- **`E`, the strong push, bursts it.** Cataclysm on the ball bursts it into a ring of air
  that throws everything it was carrying outwards. It is an early end to a ride, on her
  terms.
- **Through fire, it is lit.** A ball that passes through her fire carries the fire with
  it: a rolling ball of flame that burns whoever it is carrying (not her; her own fire never
  burns her, which the Downdraft's landing already depends on). It is proposed, not
  required.

### The rule it runs into

The jump courses gave the game a rule on 2026-10-03: *a movement tool's reach is paid for in
execution, never in waiting.* A charge is waiting. Her existing charges get away with it
because of the argument in 0004: she crawls in plain view, and an opponent walking at her
eats the gap, so in a fight a charge is paid for in distance. On a jump course there is no
opponent, so a charged ride she takes herself is reach bought by standing still.

Four ways out, and this note leans towards the second:

1. **Accept it.** The rule was written about courses, and the ball does not bridge gaps.
2. **A ball carrying somebody shrinks faster.** Carrying is paid for, so a full charge
   moves her a good distance, and the long trips come from what she does after she jumps
   off (the Updraft, the Thermal). Those are execution.
3. **She steers while riding**, and the steering is what is hard.
4. **A ball carrying her has a smaller cap than one carrying somebody else.**

### Aiming

It is two points: a place chosen at the press and a direction chosen at the release. That
is exactly the shape of `aim::racing_path` (Fissure), which runs from something she placed
earlier, flat towards the crosshair's spot on the ground. `aim.rs` says Racing is "Fissure
only", so the change is to make Racing "a thing she placed earlier, sent flat towards the
crosshair's ground spot", and the Air ball uses it. The press uses `aim::grounded_path`.
No ray-against-shape work goes anywhere near the ability.

### Starting knobs (guesses, for the first play)

| --- | --- | --- |

## 3 · The Updraft as a takeoff: space and right click

- **From the floor**, space and right click is the Updraft as built: a column of air on her
  own body that lifts her and everyone in it, each by their own weight.
- **In the air**, holding space with right click is the same Updraft, once per trip, as the
  Champion's in-air takeoff is. **This is what stretches a jump.**
- **It keeps her sideways speed.** The column only ever pushes up, so a running takeoff
  stays a running takeoff, and a jump off the Air ball keeps the ball's speed. In the code
  the column already only sets upward speed. What has to change is that the move must not
  slow her through its own startup, because a takeoff that brakes her is not one that
  stretches a jump.

### The Thermal: where her wind and her fire meet

**A Thermal is what happens whenever her Updraft and her own fire share space, whichever
came first:**

- **Updraft into fire:** she takes off inside a pillar's base, the tornado, an ember cloud,
  or the Fire carpet.
- **Fire into the Updraft:** while the column is still blowing, she lays the Fire carpet
  into it.

A Thermal lifts her roughly twice as high as an Updraft does. **If the fire is the Fire
carpet, it also pushes her along the carpet's length**, which is the forward boost the
person asked for. The carpet points where she was looking when she laid it, so she chooses
the direction of the boost when she lays it. Anyone else in the column is lifted and lit.

**A Thermal uses up the carpet.** The heat goes up with her. That stops one carpet from
being a booster pad she can use again and again, and it means the carpet can be spent once
on a Thermal or many times on lit shots, but not both.

The one-takeoff-per-trip rule matters here. The Updraft is the takeoff, so a trip that
started on a different takeoff cannot Updraft in the air. Starting from an ordinary jump
keeps it free.

## 4 · The Fire carpet: middle click in the air

**What it is.** A sheet of fire she lays out in front of her in the air. It hangs where she
put it, and anyone who touches it burns. Two jobs:

1. **Shoot through it.** A Gale (or an Air bolt) that passes through it comes out lit, the
   stronger fire disc.
2. **Updraft into it.** A Thermal, forwards and up, along the carpet.

**The recommended shape: a carpet laid along her aim.** A strip about two metres wide,
starting a metre or two in front of her and running five or six metres out along where she
is looking, tilted with her pitch.

One shape does both jobs. A Gale fired straight ahead flies *along* the carpet, through
fire the whole way, so it is lit with nothing to line up. An Updraft whose column reaches
the near end of the carpet throws her up and along it. And it reads as a red carpet,
literally: a path laid out ahead of her that she then takes.

**The alternative: a wall across her aim.** An upright sheet a few metres ahead, square to
where she is looking. Shots go through it; it blocks sight a little and is a real obstacle
to whoever is on the other side. It is better defence and a worse path: the Thermal would
push her *through* it rather than along it, which is shorter. If the carpet reads badly
from the opponent's seat, this is the fallback.

**The numbers the combos need.**

- **It lasts at least a second (60 frames), and no more than two.** A Gale has an 18-frame
  wind-up and then a few frames to reach the carpet. With the carpet's own recovery in
  front of that, a Gale thrown as soon as she is free needs the fire to still be there
  about forty frames after it appeared. A second gives slack for a human; two seconds is
  as long as a field she can lay in the air should stay before it is terrain.
- **One at a time.** A second carpet replaces the first, as a second pillar does.
- **It hangs her a little** (an ordinary aerial's hang), and is not a refuel by itself.
  The refuel is the Thermal it makes possible.

**The two combos, in full:**

> **The lit Gale:** in the air, **middle click** (the carpet ahead) → **right click** (the
> Gale) straight down the carpet → it comes out lit. Two presses, about a second.

> **The boost:** jump → **middle click** (the carpet ahead and slightly up) → **space and
> right click** as she reaches its near end → a Thermal throws her up and along it. Or
> the other way round: **space and right click** first, then **middle click** into the
> column while it blows.

## 5 · The fire takeoff: space and middle click

The person has not specified this slot. Two proposals:

**Fire fountain.** *Recommended.* As in 0007: she leaves the floor on a short column of
flame that hits all round her. It is the *hit on the way up*, and it leaves fire at her
takeoff spot. Because a takeoff is once per trip, this trip cannot Updraft in the air. The
fire it leaves is for the trip after: land back in it, Updraft from the floor, Thermal.

**Phoenix trail.** She rises in fire and leaves an upright ribbon of flame along the path
she rose through: a vertical carpet. A Gale fired back down through it is lit. It gives
less damage than the fountain and more setup.

Neither one is the Thermal's way up from the floor. That is already in the grid: **a Fire
pillar at her own feet, then space and right click in its base.** Make the fire, then use
it, with two buttons, and it costs a pillar's 17-frame wind-up standing still. That is the
"not necessarily easy" way the person allowed for.

## 6 · What moves, what goes, what is open

**Moves.**

| Move | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raise, Fissure | `E` tap, `E` hold | Left click tap, hold |
| Fire pillar, Strike | `Q` tap, `Q` hold | Middle click tap, hold |
| Bolt | Left click on foot | `Q` on foot |
| Cataclysm | Right click on foot | `E` on foot |
| Air bolt | Left click in the air | `Q` in the air |
| Updraft | `F` on foot | Space and right click, from the floor or once per trip in the air |
| Gale | Right click in the air | Unchanged |

**Goes.** Cinder spray. Its two jobs, fire for shots to fly through and fire to land in,
are now the carpet's and the pillar's. If the person wants it kept, it could take `F` on
foot.

**The Downdraft.** It is on `F` in the air today and can stay there. 0007's proposal (a
Gale aimed at the floor just below her becomes the Downdraft) still works, and frees `F`
entirely; this note leaves it to the person.

**Open, the person's:** the earth takeoff (space and left click) and the earth aerial (left
click in the air). Where they plug in:

- They are the Raise and Fissure's row, so they should do something with stones.
- Landfall (a dive that drives a slab up out of the floor ahead) is the built candidate for
  the earth aerial. If it moves to left click in the air, `E` in the air wants a strong push
  of its own.
- The stone jump (jumping while a stone is still rising under her) stays as it is. It is the
  class's biggest movement, and nothing in this note competes with it on height; the Air
  ball competes with it on distance along the ground.

## 8 · What building it touches

- `state::elementalist_move` and the keyed moves: the new map, and the takeoff window
  moved out of the Champion (0007 §8, item 1).
- Two new effects in the fixed effect array: the ball and the carpet, one of each at a
  time. The ball carries bodies, which is new for an effect. The Fire pillar's
  footprint-and-column and the Updraft's column are the nearest existing shapes.
- `aim.rs`: Racing generalised from Fissure to "a thing she placed, sent flat"; the
  carpet's placement is a planted shape along the look, a cousin of `aim::planted_ahead`.
- The Thermal: one overlap test between her Updraft column and her fire, the same kind of
  test as `World::land_the_draft`'s "is she in fire".
- Oven knobs for every number in §2 and §4, and `bake_tuning` after.
- Tests: the ball's distance grows with the square of its size; a ride drops its rider
  before the ball vanishes; a Gale thrown at once after the carpet comes out lit; a
  Thermal off the carpet carries her along it and uses it up; the Updraft takeoff keeps
  her sideways speed.
- Clips: the ball's charge and release, the carpet, the Updraft as a takeoff. The pillar
  and Fissure keep their clips; only the button changed.
