# Skill ideas

- Disk skillshot; short range; throws out a small disk that enlarges if it hits an enemy; when enlarged the disk will spin rapidly, dealing damage in three rapid pulses that push enemies back
  - The pushback means that this is useful for peel
  - Damage increases if enemies persist in moving towards you or if enemies are falling towards you
- Knocks enemies aside to the left and right of your forward path, dashes forward after
