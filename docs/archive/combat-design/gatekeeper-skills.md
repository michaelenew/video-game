# Gatekeeper skills

## Notes

- The gatekeeper as a ranged physical damage dealer is hard to fit into the rest of the game because their weapon choices are limited to bows, which no other class uses
- A realistic bow playstyle means that we cannot impose a range on the bow
  - Reasonable DPS at any range means that it's nearly impossible to balance against other classes
  - Could impose an artificial range by giving arcana arrows a limited life span
- Gates are very difficult to use since they involve positions and orientations
- Probably needs a total overhaul
  - Needs to stay ranged
  - Might be easier to implement a knife-throwing kind of playstyle where knives have an arc that stops them from having very high range

## Skills

- **Arcane fletching** — arrows from arcana, very low consumption and does not affect regen
- Arrow that implodes upon being reactivated, sucking everything towards it (including the keeper) and damaging them

### Gates

- Acceleration
- **Teleportation** — first cast casts the entry portal
  - If an enemy collides with the entry portal the exit portal will automatically spawn directly above the entry
- Reflection
- Multiplication

### Gate combos

- Teleport + acceleration
- Gravitation + multiplication + (acceleration)
- Teleport + multiplication + (acceleration)
- Gravitation + reflection
