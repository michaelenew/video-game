# Elementalist skills

## Fire

*Designed to deal damage at the expense of resource management; major synergies with other abilities to allow players to make every cast count.*

- **Ring of fire** — lays a circular area that burns for a few seconds, dealing damage to nearby enemies
  - Drops the structure as a charged area that explodes after 5 seconds or on enemy contact
- **Fire pillar** — creates a focused pillar of fire at target location; small central pillar and moderate range AoE around it
  - High damage, central pillar staggers
  - Cast on whirlwind to create a firestorm
- **Flame spitter** — channels fire towards cursor location with high range
  - With structure → melts the structure into a magma field that damages and slows
  - Damage increases further out
- **Conflagrate** — hits enemies in a small circular area
  - Applies burn that deals damage based on current health
- **Spitfire** — five small projectiles form around the elementalist in a vertical ring; when released they will all move to the target location; target location is determined by the duration the skill key is held and releasing sends the projectiles flying
  - With structure → breaks the boulder into five pieces; pieces will stagger if they hit an enemy in rapid succession
  - Without structure the projectiles will explode any structures they hit

## Ice / air

- **Frost sprite** — summons a frost sprite that puffs a mild healing AoE around the elementalist
  - **Frost sprite's domain** — creates a sentinel stone that heals allies and reflects projectiles; this will only take effect if a structure is in the area of effect of the frost sprite
  - Cast on an icy area to send the frost sprite into a territorial frenzy, dealing high damage over time and lowering enemy resistances
- **Crystallize** — swirling AoE around caster, deals damage over time after an initial pushback of enemies
  - Structures in the initial effect will be taken up, floating around the caster, blocking spells that hit them; these structures can be used to augment other casts
- **Whirlwind** — powerful suction AoE at target location, leaves an icy area, medium damage stationary tornado
- **Air ball** — sends a ball of swirling air that moves forward while tracking toward the cursor, gaining speed as it goes; the ball will explode after hitting an enemy, dealing moderate damage and knocking enemies away from the blast
  - Knockback is based on ball speed
  - Can be manually detonated by recasting the ability
  - This ability can knock back the elementalist casting it
- **Ice blast** — medium range, fast casting, cone skillshot; knocks back structures it hits, dealing extra damage and staggering enemies they hit
- **Gust** — after a short delay summons a gust of wind which lifts nearby enemies into the air (lighter enemies = higher lift) (this ability can be dodged by rolling as the wind comes through)
  - Structures hit by the gust will circle around the elementalist; these can block damage and be used by other abilities

## Earth

*Designed to limit enemy movements and enhance ally movements.*

- **Fissure** — skillshot that moves quickly forward through the ground to medium/long range and stops at the first enemy hit, knocking them back; leaves an AoE slow where the skillshot traverses that lasts for 10 seconds and a structure at the location of the hit
- Causes a structure to erupt from the ground in front of the elementalist which falls down at the target location, dealing high damage and staggering
- **Quake** — small AoE (3m diameter); the area will immediately shake, causing any moving enemies to stagger and after a short delay the ground will erupt, dealing moderate damage to all in the AoE and leaving a structure in the center
- **Rock spike** — spike that raises from the ground, dealing damage before retreating
  - Cast on structure to send out a higher damage spike from the structure
- **Blockade** — raises three structures in a line perpendicular to the direction the elementalist is facing from the target location on the ground, slight knockback and low damage

## Lightning

- **Chain arc** — short range, lightning skips up to 5 times, hold cast to control skips by clicking

## Proposed build 1

### Fire

*Casting fire abilities gives stacking counters on the elementalist that increase the cost of all abilities.*

- Base mechanics
  - **Fire** → ranged fireball that deals damage
    - With structure → raises the stone into the air, fires meteor on click release
  - Hitting a structure with a fire ability will cause the structure to catch on fire; the next time the structure is hit with a damaging ability or violently displaced (eg with a knockback) it will explode and deal damage to nearby enemies
- Ring of fire
- Conflagrate
- Fire pillar
- Flame spitter
- Spitfire

### Ice / air

*Double jump height and slightly increased movement speed.*

- Base mechanics
  - **Fire** → short range air blast that pushes back enemies it hits
- Crystallize
- Whirlwind
- Ice blast
- Gust

### Earth

- Base mechanics
  - Abilities leave behind structures, which are essentially just boulders (some are differently shaped though)
  - **Fire** → slight knockback for structures near your cursor; greater knockback for structures in the air; knockback decreases with higher range
- Fissure
- Quake
- Rock spike
