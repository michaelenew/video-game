Guard — 120° arc, slow turn
Parry — first 4 frames of guard
Stun — block, hit, or staggered
Guard covers a frontal arc, not a bubble, and turning is slow while you hold it — walk around a blocking opponent and hit the back. Blocking takes no chip damage; it costs knockback and a window where you cannot act. The first four frames of a guard parry instead, staggering the attacker for 34 frames.

Frame data comes from MOVES in crates/sim/src/state.rs — Bash is 4/3/10, Slam is 14/4/24. The checksum is what peers compare to detect desync.
